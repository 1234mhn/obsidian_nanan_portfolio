## 📌 重点词汇/短语

### ① scroll (through) — 刷手机/页面

💬 **本课用法：**
> I lose ten minutes **scrolling** before I even notice.

🔄 **替换表达：**
· 简单版：look through
· 常用版：**scroll through** social media
· 升级版：**mindlessly scroll** / **get sucked into scrolling**

🏷️ **同类词：**
· swipe — 滑（左右滑）
· tap — 点
· browse — 浏览（更正式）

🎯 **场景拓展：**
· 日常：I was scrolling through Instagram before bed.
· 吐槽：I spent an hour scrolling and learned nothing.

### ② reach for — 伸手拿

💬 **本课用法：**
> Sometimes I **reach for** my phone on the bedside table.

🔄 **替换表达：**
· 简单版：pick up
· 常用版：**grab** my phone
· 升级版：**fumble for** my phone（摸黑找手机）

🏷️ **相关词：**
· bedside table / nightstand — 床头柜
· alarm goes off — 闹钟响了
· snooze — 贪睡

🎯 **场景拓展：**
· 日常：I reach for my coffee as soon as I wake up.
· 旅行：I reached for my phone to check the time in the hotel.

### ③ glued shut — 粘住了睁不开

💬 **本课用法：**
> My eyes feel like they're **glued** shut.

🔄 **替换表达：**
· 简单版：My eyes won't open.
· 常用版：My eyes feel **heavy**.
· 升级版：My eyelids feel like they're **glued together**.

🏷️ **同类用法：**
· glued to the screen — 离不开屏幕
· glued to the spot — 呆在原地

🎯 **场景拓展：**
· 日常：After a long day, I'm just glued to the couch.
· 工作：He was glued to his computer all afternoon.

## 🔧 今日修正

**1.** `Something I'm learning machine learning.`
→ 去掉 "Something"。直接说 **"I've been learning machine learning."**

**2.** `I think machine learning is very difficult to.`
→ 结尾的 `to` 多了。**"I think machine learning is very difficult."**

**3.** `I just. Try it. I just. Try to my best.`
→ 不要在 `just` 后面断句。**"I just try my best."**

**4.** `I woke up at I woke up about seven seven at seven.`
→ 重复了。**"I woke up at around seven."**

**5.** `I usually open my mind`
→ 想说"睁眼" = **open my eyes**，不是 open my mind

**6.** `reach out my phone on the 床头柜`
→ **reach for**（伸手够），不是 reach out
→ 床头柜 = **bedside table** / **nightstand**

**7.** `check my how many followers follow me`
→ **"Check how many followers I have."**

**8.** `学科一个科目吧`
→ **"It's a subject."** / **"It's a field of study."**

## ⚠️ 今日易错点

| 类型 | 例子 | 建议 |
|:----|:-----|:-----|
| 介词 | try **to** my best / reach **out** | try my best / reach **for** |
| 句子不完整 | difficult **to** ___ | 想好再说 |
| 中文混入 | 床头柜、学科 | 用英语描述代替 |

**Part 1 — My Morning Routine**

I usually wake up at around seven in the morning. But honestly, my eyes always feel glued shut — I never want to get out of bed! Sometimes I reach for my phone on the bedside table and start scrolling. I check my messages, and I also check how many new followers I got. After scrolling for a while, I finally get up, go to the bathroom, wash my face, and brush my teeth. It's a simple routine, but it helps me start the day.

**Part 2 — Learning Machine Learning**

Lately, I've been learning machine learning. To be honest, it's really hard. I don't always know how to learn it or how to apply it. But I just try my best. I think learning something new is always difficult at first — you just have to keep going. I'm not giving up yet!
